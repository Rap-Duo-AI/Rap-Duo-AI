## How to Make an AI Rap Video from Photos

### 1. Add the two people

Open the [Rap Duo AI creator](https://rapduoai.org/#create). Choose **Two photos** for separate portraits or **One photo of both** for a shared picture. Upload JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC files, up to **20 MB per photo**. HEIC images are converted automatically.

Choose photos with clear faces and good lighting. Separate portraits do not need to share a background or be taken at the same time.

### 2. Pick a stage

Keep **Hotel Lobby (orange)** for the orange-studio look, or select another setting. Expand the stage selector to see all eight options.

### 3. Choose the video settings

Start with the default **Mini · 12 seconds · 720p · 9:16**, or adjust the model, length, quality and format. The creator displays the credit cost for that configuration.

### 4. Give the song a topic

The topic is optional. For a personal song, name the occasion and add one or two useful details. A short clip works best with a focused idea that leaves room for the duet.

For example:

> Alex and Jamie are celebrating Alex's graduation. Mention the late-night study sessions and their first road trip. Keep the rap upbeat, friendly and in English.

This is an example you can edit, not a fixed script or a restriction on who can appear. Replace the names and details with your own. If you leave the topic empty, the system chooses a general subject without those personal details.

### 5. Generate, watch and download

Sign in, review the credit cost or the free-preview eligibility shown by the creator, and submit. Track the saved work in [My videos](https://rapduoai.org/settings/works). You can leave the page while a submitted video processes.

Paid videos open with full playback and download. If your request qualifies for a free preview, you can use credits to unlock that same complete video. Download your result before its displayed expiry date.

## Tips for a More Personal Rap Duet

- **Start with recognizable faces.** Good lighting and clear, unobstructed faces give the creator a useful reference. Avoid heavy filters, masks or sunglasses that hide important features.
- **Keep the story focused.** A name, an occasion and one shared detail are more useful for a short song than a long biography.
- **Put personal facts in the topic.** If a name, birthday or anniversary matters, write it explicitly. A stage supplies scenery, not the full story.
- **State the lyric language when it matters.** You can request a language in the topic. Otherwise, the topic's main language guides the song; empty or ambiguous topics follow the English or Chinese site language.
- **Choose a format before generating.** Vertical 9:16 is a practical starting point for short-form mobile video; square and landscape options are also available.
- **Listen to the demos.** Compare actual vocals, rhythm and the interaction between the performers before choosing your settings.
- **Review and save the result.** Check names, pronunciation and visual details, then download the video before it expires.

AI output can vary in likeness, movement, wording and pronunciation. Clear input helps communicate your intent, but it does not guarantee an exact performance.

## Rap Duo AI FAQ

### What is Rap Duo AI?

Rap Duo AI is a browser-based AI rap video generator for two people. It uses two portraits or one shared photo to create a short duet with generated lyrics, vocals, a beat and a video performance.

### Can I use two separate photos?

Yes. Upload one portrait of each person, or switch to the single-photo option for a picture of both people. Separate portraits can have different backgrounds.

### Do I need to sing, film myself or upload audio?

No. The creator works from photos and an optional topic. It generates the song and video together. There is no audio-recording upload step.

### Will the video use our real voices?

The voices are AI-generated for the performance. A photo does not clone your real voice, and the creator does not offer an artist voice selector.

### Can I make a birthday rap video with names?

Yes. Add the names and occasion in the topic, along with a short personal detail. You can choose the Birthday party stage or any other setting. Exact wording and pronunciation can vary.

### Can I choose the song's language?

Request a language in the topic. If you do not specify one, the topic's main language guides the lyrics. If the topic is empty or contains only ambiguous details such as names or emoji, the current English or Chinese website language is used as guidance. Language accuracy is not guaranteed.

### Is Hotel Lobby AI a separate tool?

The [Hotel Lobby AI page](https://rapduoai.org/hotel-lobby-ai) uses the same Rap Duo AI creator with the orange-stage focus. It follows the same model options, credit costs and delivery rules.

### Can I make a 30-second rap video?

The Ultra model includes 20- and 30-second options. Mini, Fast and Pro offer lengths up to 15 seconds. The creator displays the cost of the selected duration before submission.

### How long does generation take?

Processing time varies. Follow the work in My videos; there is no fixed completion-time promise. A task that does not complete within the 10-minute limit fails and returns the credits charged for that attempt.

### Can I close the browser while it generates?

Yes, after the work has been submitted and saved. Sign in to the same account and return to My videos, including from another device, to check it.

### Is Rap Duo AI free?

There is no unrestricted free plan. Some first-use requests qualify for a limited, watermarked preview under the current eligibility rules. Other requests use credits, available through one-time packs or subscriptions. Check the creator for your actual eligibility.

### Does unlocking create a different video?

No. Unlocking opens the same complete video behind your preview. It does not generate a replacement song. Paid generation provides the complete video directly.

### Can I download a video without a watermark?

Paid and unlocked videos can be downloaded as full MP4 files without the Rap Duo AI preview watermark. Download while the work is still within its storage period.

### What happens if generation fails?

Credits deducted for a confirmed failed paid generation are returned automatically. The service does not automatically submit another generation. If a delivered file has a technical problem, contact support with the work details.

### Are purchases refundable?

The published policy accepts eligible refund requests within 7 days of successful payment. Eligibility depends on the purchase type and whether its credits have been used. Read the [Refund Policy](https://rapduoai.org/refund-policy) for the exact conditions; a failed-generation credit return is separate from a purchase refund.

### Can I cancel a subscription?

Yes. Manage future renewals in [Billing](https://rapduoai.org/settings/billing). Canceling renewal keeps the current paid period available until expiry; cancellation does not itself issue a purchase refund.

### Do I need permission to use someone's photo?

Yes. Use images you own or are authorized to use, with permission from the people shown. Review the output before sharing and make clear that it is AI-generated where context requires. See the [Terms of Service](https://rapduoai.org/terms-of-service).

### Where can I get help?

Sign in and open a [support ticket](https://rapduoai.org/settings/tickets) for account, payment or video questions. You can also contact [hi@rapduoai.org](mailto:hi@rapduoai.org).
